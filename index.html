Our Story

A Taste of Thailand in Fairy Meadow

We bring the vibrant flavours of Thailand to the Illawarra, with dishes crafted from traditional recipes and the freshest local ingredients. Whether you're joining us for a relaxed lunch or an evening out, every meal is prepared with care and served with warmth.

Dhok Koon Thai Restaurant has been a beloved part of Fairy Meadow's dining scene, serving authentic Thai food to the Wollongong and Illawarra community. From fragrant green curries and rich Massaman to classic Pad Thai and Tom Yum soup, our menu celebrates the full spectrum of Thai cuisine. We're located at 1/23–25 Princes Highway, Fairy Meadow — just minutes from Wollongong CBD, North Wollongong, Balgownie and Towradgi. Whether you're after a quick Thai takeaway lunch, a relaxed BYO dinner with friends, or delivery to your door, Dhok Koon is your local Thai restaurant of choice.
